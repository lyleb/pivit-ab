Cookies on the public pages

This section covers the public pivitlab pages: the homepage, this page, and the terms page. The sign-in pages, the dashboard, and the experiment snippet customers paste into their own sites do not show this banner and do not load these tags.

Before any Google script runs, those pages set Google Consent Mode to denied for analytics_storage, ad_storage, ad_user_data and ad_personalization. Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity are not loaded, and nothing is sent to Google or Microsoft, until you click Accept.

Accept calls gtag('consent','update',{analytics_storage:'granted'}). Advertising storage stays denied. The browser then loads Google Tag Manager, container GTM-KZCDC3HJ. That container contains Google Analytics 4, measurement id G-NT78JHVHGY, and Microsoft Clarity. Reject loads nothing.

The choice is stored in this browser under the localStorage key pivitlab_analytics_consent. That is not a cookie. The values are granted and denied. Nothing is written until you click. The code sets no expiry. It stays until you clear site data for this browser, or you choose again from Cookie settings. Choosing Reject after Accept sets consent back to denied and deletes the _ga, _ga_*, _clck and _clsk cookies where the browser allows.
