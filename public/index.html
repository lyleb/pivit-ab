Bayesian Analysis (which variant is actually winning, and how sure we can be)

        
Instead of just a raw percentage, this treats each variant's true conversion rate as uncertain and narrows that uncertainty as data comes in — so a 20% lead from 10 visitors and a 20% lead from 10,000 visitors don't look equally convincing. "Probability best" is the chance that variant is genuinely the top performer, not just ahead by chance.
